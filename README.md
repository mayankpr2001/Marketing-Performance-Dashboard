# 📊 Marketing Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## 1. Project Title / Headline

**Marketing Performance Dashboard – Campaign Analytics & ROI Insights**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze marketing campaign performance, advertising spend, revenue generation, and conversion funnel metrics across multiple platforms.

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

### 📌 About the Project

This project focuses on analyzing marketing campaign data to understand how effectively advertising budgets are being utilized and how different campaigns and platforms contribute to business results.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key marketing KPIs, including total spend, impressions, clicks, leads, applications, enrollments, revenue, and Return on Ad Spend (ROAS).

It helps marketers and business stakeholders identify high-performing campaigns, compare advertising channels, monitor conversion rates, and make data-driven marketing decisions.

**Project Objective:** To transform raw marketing data into meaningful insights through interactive visualizations and performance tracking.

## 3. Tech Stack

- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and interactive data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPI calculations and performance metrics
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data handling and preparation
- **Data Visualization** – Funnel charts, bar charts, line charts, KPI cards, and tables

## 4. Data Source

**Source: Kaggle**

The project uses marketing campaign performance data sourced from Kaggle.

The dataset contains campaign-level and channel-level information used to analyze advertising expenditure, impressions, leads, revenue, and campaign effectiveness.


## 5. Features and Highlights

### 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks the following metrics:

- **Total Spend:** 91.45M
- **Total Impressions:** 129M
- **Total Clicks:** 7M
- **Total Leads:** 1M
- **Total Applications:** 784K
- **Total Enrollments:** 392K
- **Total Revenue:** 2.17bn

### 🔍 Marketing Conversion Funnel

Visualizes the marketing journey from impressions to enrollments, helping users understand the conversion process and identify potential drop-off points.

### 🎯 Campaign Performance Analysis

Compares five campaigns:

- UG Admission
- MBA Admission
- Placements
- Semester Exchange Program
- Hackathon

Evaluates campaign-level spend, leads, revenue, and ROAS to identify stronger-performing campaigns.

### 📢 Channel Spend and Revenue Analysis

Compares the performance of five advertising platforms:

- Google Ads
- Facebook
- LinkedIn
- Instagram
- YouTube

Helps identify how advertising budgets and revenue are distributed across different marketing channels.

### 💰 Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)

Tracks ROAS across campaigns and platforms to evaluate advertising efficiency and compare revenue generated against marketing expenditure.

### 🎨 Creative Performance Analysis

Compares creative formats such as:

- Video
- Static Ads
- Carousel
- Reels

Provides a visual comparison of spend and revenue associated with different creative formats.

### 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for:

- Date
- Campaign Name
- Platform
- Region
- Manager

These filters allow users to explore performance across different marketing dimensions.

### 📋 Detailed Performance Tables

Includes campaign-level and platform-level summaries with total spend, leads, revenue, and ROAS for more detailed comparisons.

## 📌 Key Insights

- The dashboard reports approximately **2.17bn in total revenue** against **91.45M in marketing spend**.
- The Placements campaign records the highest campaign ROAS displayed in the dashboard, at approximately **25.45**.
- LinkedIn records the highest platform ROAS displayed, at approximately **25.45**.
- The funnel shows 129M impressions and 392K enrollments, highlighting the importance of tracking conversion throughout the marketing journey.
- Comparing creative formats and advertising channels helps identify opportunities for further campaign analysis and budget optimization.


## 🚀 Project Outcome

This project demonstrates practical skills in Power BI dashboard development, data transformation, KPI reporting, marketing analytics, and data storytelling.

It showcases how interactive dashboards can turn campaign data into actionable insights that support performance monitoring and informed marketing decisions.
